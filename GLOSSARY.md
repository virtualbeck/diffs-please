# Diffs, Please

A game in which the player supervises AI coding agents by choosing skills and judging their work. This glossary names the parts of the game, not the software terms the game teaches.

## Language

### Structure

**Day**:
A themed set of cases that ends in a token report.
_Avoid_: Level, chapter

**Case**:
One visitor's situation, played from briefing to lesson.
_Avoid_: Level, submission, round

**Briefing**:
The opening page of a case: who arrived, what they said, and what is going on.
_Avoid_: Intro, setup

**Step**:
One decision the player makes inside a case. There are four kinds: skill pick, choice, mismatch and stamp.
_Avoid_: Question, turn

**Page**:
One screenful. Everything the player needs for the current step is on it, and it never scrolls.
_Avoid_: Screen, view

**Lesson**:
The closing line of a case, under "What the docs say", traceable to the upstream skill docs.
_Avoid_: Moral, tip

### Steps

**Skill pick**:
A step where the player chooses which skill to run from a shortlist.
_Avoid_: Skill drawer, loadout

**Choice**:
A step where the player answers a question the skill has raised.
_Avoid_: Quiz, multiple choice

**Mismatch**:
A two-part step where the player finds the agreement that was not kept, then the change that breaks it.
_Avoid_: Tap, discrepancy, diff hunt

**Stamp**:
The final, one-shot verdict on finished work: merge, send back or reject.
_Avoid_: Decision, vote

### Scoring

**Burn**:
Tokens spent. Running a skill burns tokens and so does every mistake. Nothing earns them back.
_Avoid_: Cost, pay, score, earn

**Avoidable burn**:
Burn caused by a mistake. It is the measure of how well the player did.
_Avoid_: Penalty, waste

**Budget**:
The tokens available for one day.
_Avoid_: Balance, wallet

**Context**:
How full the agent's working memory is. It fills as tokens burn and empties when the player clears or hands off.
_Avoid_: Energy, health

**Citation**:
The notice a player receives for a stamp that lets bad work through or blocks good work.
_Avoid_: Strike, fine

**Token report**:
The end-of-day summary of budget, burn, avoidable burn and citations.
_Avoid_: Ledger, pay slip

### Reference

**Cheat sheet**:
The reference the player can open at any time: a hint, the player's glossary, the flows and one page per skill.
_Avoid_: Help, manual

**Highlight**:
A marked phrase in an explanation: the action to take, the verdict, the habit, or the term being defined.
_Avoid_: Bold, emphasis
